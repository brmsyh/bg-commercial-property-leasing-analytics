# Executive summary: leasing performance 2021–2025

**To:** Board and asset management · **As of:** 31 December 2025

**In one line:** we are not short of demand; we lose tenants we already have through slow repairs, and we keep space empty by asking above market.

### Where we stand
- 94.0% occupied, EUR 105.0M net rent in 2025, 99.4% of 2025 billing collected, 7.7% gross yield.
- But 6 of 26 buildings hold 52% of all vacant space.

### Three causes, three owners

| | What the data shows | Cost | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Service** | Tenants with 3+ repair SLA breaches renew at 24% (vs 73% with none). Two buildings breach SLA on 2 of every 3 tickets, mostly HVAC. | ~EUR 0.67M rent / year | Facility management |
| **Pricing** | Four buildings ask 15–23% above market and need 8.5–12.4 months to re-let (vs 4.7). | ~EUR 0.70M rent / year | Asset management |
| **Cash** | 53% of 180+ day arrears is the disputed 2022 service-charge true-up in four retail centres. | EUR 0.62M tied up | Finance + leasing |

### Decisions requested
1. Approve an HVAC remediation and SLA recovery plan for the two failing buildings.
2. Authorise re-pricing of vacant units in four buildings to within 5% of the market benchmark.
3. Mandate commercial settlement (instalment plans tied to extensions) of the reconciliation dispute.
4. Leasing to start retention talks now with the **51 high-risk tenants expiring in 2026 (EUR 1.6M rent p.a.)**.

### What we will track monthly
Occupancy by building · months to re-let · SLA breach rate · renewal rate · arrears > 90 days.
